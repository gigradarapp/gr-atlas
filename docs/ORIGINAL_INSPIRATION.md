# Original Inspiration

**Recorded:** 15 August 2026

This note preserves the original idea that connected the existing Buzo consumer product to the Buzo Atlas operator layer.

## Initial concept

> Buzo is an AI nightlife concierge with an operator layer for family offices investing in hospitality, venues, festivals and entertainment assets. It consolidates bespoke asset reports into one risk view while consumer activity provides demand signals.

## What this preserves

- The consumer nightlife concierge
- The team's nightlife founder-market fit
- The existing event-ingestion and recommendation product
- A new B2B dashboard for asset performance and risk

This is the original inspiration, not a claim that every component is already implemented or validated. The current product direction may evolve while this note remains a record of where Buzo Atlas began.
